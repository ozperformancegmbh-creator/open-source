## 2. Which sources Google and AI assistants cite for German car-repair/transmission questions

### Takeaway
In Germany in autumn 2026, YouTube, Google properties and German Wikipedia lead Google's AI surfaces, Reddit is top-3, and ChatGPT's source mix is different (YouTube absent from its German top 50). No study was found that breaks out automotive/transmission queries or measures Motor-Talk, ADAC or model forums — that part is evidence-free and must be tested by hand.

### Cited Findings
- Google AI Mode, Germany, October 2026 (Ahrefs Brand Radar): youtube.com most-cited domain with 15.82% of mentions, google.com 14.07%, de.wikipedia.org 13.81%. — [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/google-ki-modus-deutschland-meistzitierte-domains/)
- AI Mode vs AI Overviews in Germany: 34 of the top-50 cited domains are on both lists. — [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ki-modus-vs-ai-overviews-quellen/)
- ChatGPT, Germany, August 2026: YouTube is the most-cited domain in Google AI Overviews but does not appear in ChatGPT's German top 50; Reddit is in the top 3 on both platforms (attribution of the Reddit statement to this specific comparison is from a search summary). — [Ahrefs DE AIO vs ChatGPT](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-overviews-vs-chatgpt-quellen/); [Ahrefs DE ChatGPT top 50](https://ahrefs.com/blog/de/chatgpt-deutschland-meistzitierte-domains/)
- Ahrefs stresses these are snapshots and that patterns shift. — [Ahrefs DE ChatGPT top 50](https://ahrefs.com/blog/de/chatgpt-deutschland-meistzitierte-domains/)
- Ahrefs (DE): 59.6% of pages cited in ChatGPT are "unreachable for marketers". — [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/chatgpt-zitierte-seiten/)

- Motor-Talk: moved from mobile.de to gutefrage.net in October 2023; about 3.6M unique users/month at that time; founded 2001, 63M+ posts, self-described largest European car community. — [ADZINE](https://www.adzine.de/2023/10/motor-talk-wandert-von-mobile-de-zu-gutefrage-net/); [Motor-Talk](https://www.motor-talk.de/)

### Inferences
- For Google's AI surfaces in German, YouTube is the single most valuable non-owned channel; for ChatGPT, text sources (Wikipedia-type reference pages, Reddit, publisher and forum pages) matter more. A workshop needs both: video for Google, crawlable text mentions for ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity.
- German automotive questions are long-tail and model-specific (gearbox code + symptom + fault code). Classic search results for such queries are dominated by AutoBild/ams/Autozeitung, Motor-Talk and model forums, so those are the likely grounding sources for AI answers too — but this is inference, not measured.
- Because German-language Reddit automotive communities are small compared with Motor-Talk and model forums, forum threads probably play the "Reddit role" for German repair queries. Unverified.
- Practical step: build a fixed prompt set (e.g. "DSG DQ200 ruckelt was tun", "Mechatronik Reparatur NRW", "Getriebespülung ZF 8HP Ruhrgebiet", "P17BF Reparatur") and log monthly which domains ChatGPT, Gemini/AI Mode, Perplexity and Claude cite. This replaces the missing niche study.

### Gaps
- No study found on AI citation sources for German automotive/repair queries; no data on Motor-Talk, gutefrage, ADAC, AutoBild, carwiki, golfv.de or Skoda/Seat/Audi/BMW forum citation shares.
- Full Ahrefs German top-50 lists (positions of gutefrage, motor-talk, adac, autobild, Facebook, TikTok) could not be read.

## 3. Forum and community engagement: rules and astroturfing risk

### Takeaway
Motor-Talk bans commercial and non-commercial advertising, self-promotion and link spam, and moderators treat a business link in the signature as a violation; a link via profile settings is the tolerated route. Undisclosed promotion of one's own business is unfair commercial practice under § 5a Abs. 4 UWG, with no exemption for self-promotion.

### Cited Findings
- Motor-Talk Beitragsregeln prohibit spam and advertising, including commercial and non-commercial advertising, link spam, self-promotion and repeated unwanted content; links to homepages of other forums are not allowed in posts. — [Motor-Talk Beitragsregeln](https://www.motor-talk.de/info/beitragsregeln)
- Moderator statements in Motor-Talk threads: someone who runs a business and puts its link in the signature breaks the link rule; a profile link can be set via profile settings; links that "smell of advertising" are deleted; moderators admit the line between helpful link and advertising is hard to draw. (Forum posts, some old; not binding rule text.) — [Links in Signatur](https://www.motor-talk.de/forum/links-in-signatur-t486524.html); [Linkregeln thread](https://www.motor-talk.de/forum/erklaert-mir-die-linkregeln-bitte-t2302764.html)
- Motor-Talk terms of use exist as a separate document (section on user duties/advertising). — [Motor-Talk Nutzungsbedingungen](https://www.motor-talk.de/info/nutzungsbedingungen)
- § 5a Abs. 4 UWG (non-disclosure of commercial purpose): the media-law carve-out (§ 6 DDG) only blocks UWG for advertising in favour of a third-party company, not for commercial acts in favour of one's own business; a label such as "Produktempfehlung" does not sufficiently disclose the advertising purpose. — [omsels.info § 5a UWG](https://www.omsels.info/die-verbote-oder-was-darf-ich-nicht/3-5a-abs-6-uwg-verschleierung-des-kommerziellen-zwecks); [omsels.info Internetbeiträge](https://www.omsels.info/die-verbote-oder-was-darf-ich-nicht/3-5a-abs-6-uwg-verschleierung-des-kommerziellen-zwecks/internetbeitraege)
- OLG Bamberg, Beschluss 24.10.2025, 3 U 35/25 e: a distribution partner promoting his own business activity without disclosure violated §§ 3 Abs. 1, 5a Abs. 4 UWG. — [gesetze-bayern.de](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-BECKRS-B-2025-N-44014?hl=true)

### Inferences
- Workable model: one clearly named expert account ("OZ Performance – Getriebetechnik, Bochum", real first name of the technician), website only in the profile field, no signature link, answers that solve the problem in-thread (fault code, measured values, repair path, rough cost) and no "come to us" pitch. The disclosure in the username satisfies UWG transparency and the absence of pitch satisfies forum rules. Whether Motor-Talk offers formal business/partner accounts was not confirmed.
- Pseudonymous accounts recommending the workshop, employees or friends posting "customer experiences", or paid forum seeding are both a forum-ban risk and a UWG risk (Abmahnung by competitors or Wettbewerbszentrale). Not worth it for a local business.
- The realistic payoff of forum work is being named by others: satisfied customers who document their repair in their model forum (golf forums, Skoda/Seat/Audi/BMW communities) create the third-party mentions AI systems use. Ask customers, never script or reward them (see section 8 on reviews).
- Reddit: same logic — disclose affiliation, answer substantively; self-promotion rules vary by subreddit and were not checked.

### Gaps
- Exact current wording of Motor-Talk terms (commercial accounts, sanctions) not read. Rules of golfv.de, vwgolf community, Skoda/Seat/Audi/BMW forums, r/de, r/automobil, r/Volkswagen, r/Golf_GTI not researched.
- No court decision found specifically on workshop/forum astroturfing.

## 4. YouTube / Shorts / TikTok / Instagram strategy

### Takeaway
The best available evidence is correlational but strong: in Ahrefs' 75,000-brand study, YouTube mentions were the factor most correlated with AI brand visibility (about 0.737), and YouTube is the most-cited domain in Google's German AI Mode. Hard evidence that video produces workshop leads in Germany, and data on German DSG repair channels, was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Ahrefs (75k brands, analysis dated December 2025): YouTube mentions correlate about 0.737 (Spearman) with AI visibility across ChatGPT, AI Mode and AI Overviews — highest of all factors; YouTube mention impressions about 0.717; branded web mentions 0.66–0.71; backlink volume and page count only weak. Authors stress correlation is not causation. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ahrefs-youtube-mentions-ai-visibility-brand-search)
- Ahrefs' explanation: AI Mode and AI Overviews belong to Google, YouTube's owner, and cite YouTube more than any other domain; YouTube is also training data. Reiterated in Ahrefs' Q1 2026 AI Search Benchmark (May 2026). — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260526119691/en/)
- YouTube was the most-cited domain in Google AI Mode in Germany in October 2026 (15.82%). — [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/google-ki-modus-deutschland-meistzitierte-domains/)
- German example of a workshop channel: "Die Autodoktoren" — Kfz-Meister Hans-Jürgen Faul and Holger Parsch, known from TV, show daily work in their own workshop on YouTube (listed by auto motor und sport among notable car channels). — [auto motor und sport](https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/tech-zukunft/werkstatt/die-besten-gebrauchtwagenkanaele-auf-youtube/)
- auto motor und sport publishes its own DSG explainer videos (function, maintenance, buying advice; usage tips) — the editorial competition for generic DSG video queries. — [ams video](https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/video/gebrauchtwagen/doppelkupplungsgetriebe-funktion-wartung-und-kaufberatung/)
- Handwerk guidance: a free YouTube channel builds a company's image because customers, skilled workers and apprentices search there (HWK Trier / Handwerksblatt); Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung has a guide on how craft businesses can use YouTube. — [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/die-handwerkskammern-in-deutschland/hwk-trier/themen-specials/digitales-handwerk/handwerk-4-0-film-ab-youtube-marketing); [DHZ](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=157932)
- Local-press angle exists for mechanics who start a channel (Nordkurier reported on a mechanic becoming a YouTuber; no customer results given). — [Nordkurier](https://www.nordkurier.de/regional/mueritz/mechaniker-aus-schwarz-geht-jetzt-unter-die-youtuber-1139367)
- OZ Performance already has a YouTube channel (@ozperformance18). — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ozperformance18)

### Inferences
- Feasible format for a 2–5 person shop: one "case of the week" video (3–8 min, phone on tripod, lavalier mic) — symptom, fault code read-out, opened part, cause, fix, cost range — plus 2–3 vertical cuts of the same footage for Shorts/TikTok/Reels. Roughly 2 hours per week.
- Video SEO basics (standard practice, not sourced here): title = model + gearbox code + symptom/fault code ("VW Golf 7 DQ200 ruckelt – P17BF Mechatronik Druckspeicher"), spoken brand + city in the first 30 seconds (transcripts are what LLMs read), chapters, corrected German subtitles, description with the fault codes, address and a link to the matching service page, pinned comment with booking link.
- Since "YouTube mentions" (brand named in other people's videos/transcripts) is the measured factor, guest appearances matter as much as own uploads: offer a gearbox teardown or diagnosis segment to established German automotive creators and regional tuning channels.
- TikTok/Instagram serve local reach and tuning-scene credibility more than AI citations; they are weakly crawlable. Prioritise YouTube, repurpose to the others.
- Chiptuning videos need the legal notices from section 8 (no pops-and-bangs/emissions-delete content, Eintragung mentioned).

### Gaps
- No verified list of German DSG/transmission repair YouTube channels with subscriber numbers; "Getriebe Wehrmann" and "DSG-Doktor" could not be confirmed to exist. Needs a direct YouTube search.
- No German data linking workshop video to leads or rankings; kfz-betrieb/Krafthand case studies not found.
- No evidence found on TikTok/Instagram effect on AI citations.

## 6. Brand mentions vs backlinks, and what entity building requires

### Takeaway
Across 75,000 brands, branded web mentions (0.664) and later YouTube mentions (about 0.737) correlate far more strongly with AI visibility than backlink volume; the top quartile by web mentions gets roughly 12 times the AI Overview mentions of the next quartile. For OZ the first entity task is consistency, since its public data currently disagrees on name, legal form and district.

### Cited Findings
- Ahrefs, May 2025 (75k brands): branded web mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview brand visibility; branded anchors 0.527; branded search volume 0.392; branded ad traffic 0.216 and ad cost 0.215 (weak). Top quartile for web mentions averaged 169 AI Overview mentions vs 14 for the next quartile; about 26% of brands had zero AI Overview mentions. Correlation, not causation. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/?tp=1)
- Ahrefs follow-up (December 2025; ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews): YouTube mentions about 0.737, branded web mentions 0.66–0.71, link volume and page count weak. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations); [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/)
- Ahrefs also published that websites with more traffic have more mentions (confounding factor). — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/websites-with-more-traffic-have-more-mentions/)
- Paid search signals show only weak correlation with AI Overview visibility (0.216 / 0.215). — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/?tp=1)
- OZ's current entity data is inconsistent: GmbH and UG entries under HRB 21451, site titles still saying "UG Getriebe & Motortechnik", district given as Werne or Langendreer. — [Creditreform GmbH](https://firmeneintrag.creditreform.de/44892/4050681448/OZ_PERFORMANCE_GMBH); [Creditreform UG](https://firmeneintrag.creditreform.de/44892/4050681448/OZ_PERFORMANCE_UG_HAFTUNGSBESCHRAENKT); [Kontakt](https://www.ozperformance.de/kontakt)
- Wikidata can be a strong signal for a Knowledge Panel but does not guarantee one (SEO-blog sources). — [eseospace](https://eseospace.com/blog/wikidata-and-wikipedia-for-geo/)

### Inferences
- The studies cover large brands; a local workshop will not appear in such datasets, and correlations partly reflect brand size. The direction is still usable: being named, with a consistent name, on many relevant third-party pages and in video transcripts matters more than raw link counts.
- Concrete entity checklist (standard practice, not individually sourced): (a) one canonical name string "OZ Performance GmbH" everywhere, old "UG" titles removed; (b) identical NAP on site, Google Business Profile, ZF finder, directories; (c) schema.org `AutoRepair`/`Organization` on the site with legalName, address, geo, telephone, founder/Meister as `Person`, `sameAs` pointing to Google Maps profile, YouTube, Instagram/TikTok, ProvenExpert, ZF finder entry, Northdata/register page; (d) correct Handelsregister data mirrored by Northdata/Creditreform; (e) an "Über uns" page stating verifiable facts (founding year, Meister title, certifications, number of gearboxes repaired); (f) decide the role of getriebe-bochum.com (redirect or clearly distinct) so signals are not split.
- A classic branded "Knowledge Panel" for a local business is in practice the Google Business Profile panel; a separate Knowledge Graph entity is a later-stage outcome of consistent mentions.

### Gaps
- No evidence specific to local businesses or the German market on mentions vs links.
- Northdata entry for OZ not seen; schema/sameAs status of the live site not checked (site not reachable).

Classic Google Search Ads remain the only practical paid lever, with no reliable niche CPC figure available.

## 8. Legal guard rails in Germany

### Takeaway
Naming makes and gearbox brands in words to describe what the workshop repairs is the accepted route under § 23 MarkenG; using manufacturer logos or eye-catching brand display is not (BGH "Große Inspektion für alle", 2011). Tuning advertising must not hide registration/approval consequences, reviews must be genuine with disclosed verification, and self-promotion must be recognisable. This is research, not legal advice — a specialist lawyer should check final page titles and ads.

### Cited Findings
**Trademarks (VW, Audi, ZF, Mercedes in titles/ads)**
- BGH, 14.04.2011, I ZR 33/10 "Große Inspektion für alle": if a repair shop uses a car maker's well-known word/figurative mark in an eye-catching way in advertising for inspection work on that maker's vehicles, the maker can prohibit it (case: ATU using the VW logo; identical sign for identical services; § 14 Abs. 2 Nr. 1, § 23 Nr. 3 MarkenG). — [Betriebs-Berater](https://betriebs-berater.ruw.de/wirtschaftsrecht/urteile/Werbung-einer-Reparaturwerkstatt-mit-der-Marke-eines-grossen-Automobilherstellers--Grosse-Inspektion-fuer-alle-19158); [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/bgh-werkstatt-darf-nicht-mit-vw-bildmarke-werben); [RA Plutte](https://www.ra-plutte.de/atu-darf-nicht-mit-vw-bildmarke-werben/); [full text mirror](https://lexetius.com/2011,4771)
- BGH I ZR 214/11: the well-known "Volkswagen" mark enjoys special protection; even the component "Volks" may only be used to a limited extent in advertising for car services. — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/bgh-urteil-i-zr-214-11-volkswagen-markenschutz-bild-atu)
- § 23 MarkenG permits use of another's mark as an indication of the purpose of a product or service where necessary and in line with honest practices; law-firm commentary treats "necessary" strictly, and notes for workshops that a specialisation cannot be communicated other than by naming the brand; assessment is case by case. — [§ 23 MarkenG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/markeng/__23.html); [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/nutzung-fremde-marken-werbung.html); [IT-Recht Kanzlei, Ersatzteile](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/markenrechtliche-fallstricke-werbung-ersatzteile.html)
- Advertising with authorisations/partner status one does not hold is a recurring ground for warnings. — [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/werbung-mit-autorisierungen-abmahnung.html)

**Chiptuning advertising**
- OLG Stuttgart (RaceChip, reported by AutoBild as decided 14 February 2019; Wettbewerbszentrale as claimant): advertising for tuning modules was held misleading/incomplete where many modules were delivered without Teilegutachten and the advertising did not adequately point this out; without it the vehicle's operating permit lapses and insurance cover may be lost. Case number and court sequence should be verified. — [Wettbewerbszentrale PDF](https://www.wettbewerbszentrale.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2020-04_Chip-Tuning_OLG-Stuttgart-1.pdf); [AutoBild](https://www.autobild.de/artikel/racechips-chiptuning-werbeverbot-fuer-bestimmte-tuning-chips-14594491.html)
- OLG Hamm 4 W 72/12 (parts under § 22a StVZO): a disclaimer that a part lacks approval could not cure the unlawful offer. — [Ferner-Alsdorf](https://www.ferner-alsdorf.de/wettbewerbsrecht-kein-verkauf-von-bauartgenehmigungspflichten-fahrzeugteilen-nach-%C2%A722a-stvzo-ohne-betriebserlaubnis/)
- TÜV guidance: do chiptuning only with Teilegutachten, ABE or Teiletypgenehmigung; modifications need an Änderungsabnahme/registration. — [TÜV NORD](https://www.tuev-nord.de/de/privatkunden/ratgeber/auto-und-verkehr/chiptuning/); [TÜV Rheinland](https://www.tuv.com/de/deutschland/gk/fahrzeuge_verkehr/entwicklung_typpruefung/umruestung_tuning/tuning.html)
- Driving without the required entry risks fines and loss of insurance cover; chip tuning can be a material defect on resale (OLG Hamm 28 U 186/10) and triggers seller disclosure duties. — [bussgeldkatalog.org](https://www.bussgeldkatalog.org/motortuning-chiptuning/); [IWW](https://www.iww.de/asr/autorecht/gw-handel-aufklaerungspflicht-des-verkaeufers-bei-chip-tuning-f92987)
- Emission-related interventions: a workshop blog classes removing or deactivating OPF/DPF, including software "DPF-off", as unlawful (lapse of operating permit under § 19 Abs. 2 StVZO; unlawful defeat device, § 27 EG-FGV). Provider's assessment, statutes not verified here. — [kfz-dietrich.com](https://kfz-dietrich.com/blog/partikelfilter-opf-dpf-tuning-legal/)

**Reviews, testimonials, self-promotion, influencer labelling**
- § 5b Abs. 3 UWG: whoever displays consumer reviews must state whether and how it ensures they come from real customers; UWG Anhang Nr. 23b bans claiming reviews come from actual customers without reasonable verification; Nr. 23c bans submitting or commissioning fake reviews and misrepresenting reviews (legislative materials also mention publishing only positive ones/deleting negative ones). — [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/werbung-kundenbewertungen-ohne-echtheitsverifizierung.html); [vzbv](https://www.vzbv.de/meldungen/mangelnde-transparenz-bei-online-bewertungen)
- OLG Köln, 20.12.2024, 6 U 59/24: a disclaimer about missing verification satisfies only § 5b Abs. 3 and does not cure a Nr. 23b violation. LG Karlsruhe, 11.12.2025, 14 O 41/25 KfH: the duty also applies to displayed average star ratings. — [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/werbung-kundenbewertungen-ohne-echtheitsverifizierung.html); [LG Karlsruhe PDF](https://www.vzhh.de/sites/default/files/medien/136/dokumente/2025-12-11_Urteil_LG-Karlsruhe_Verbraucherzentrale-Hamburg_Foot-Locker-Europe.com-BV_14-O-41-25-KfH.pdf)
- § 5a Abs. 4 UWG applies fully to undisclosed promotion of one's own business (see section 3; OLG Bamberg 24.10.2025, 3 U 35/25 e). — [gesetze-bayern.de](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-BECKRS-B-2025-N-44014?hl=true)
- BGH, 09.09.2021 (I ZR 90/20, I ZR 125/20, I ZR 126/20): influencer posts need an advertising label when there is consideration for the brand/product mention or the influencer acts on behalf of a company; a trailing #Werbung hashtag is described as insufficient in commentary. — [datenschutz-generator.de](https://datenschutz-generator.de/influencer-werbung); [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/digitalpolitik/bgh-urteil-werbekennzeichnung-instagram-creator-tap-tags-nicht-als-werbung-markieren)

**Impressum / data protection**
- The Impressum duty is now in § 5 DDG (which replaced the TMG); craft businesses with a website generally fall under it; craft-specific items include the competent chamber, statutory professional title and the state that conferred it, and reference to professional rules; information must be easily recognisable, directly reachable and permanently available; information on consumer dispute resolution is recommended. ZDH and HWK Dortmund publish a "Praxis Recht Impressumspflicht" guide (update June 2025). — [ZDH](https://www.zdh.de/fachbereiche/organisation-und-recht/praxis-recht/praxis-recht-impressumspflicht/); [HWK Dortmund PDF](https://www.hwk-do.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/20250618_2_Praxis_Recht_Impressumspflicht_Update_Juni_2025.pdf); [e-recht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/impressum/13197-impressum-handwerker.html)
- Agency and craft business are both generally liable for wrongly integrated cookie banners or incomplete privacy notices. — [e-recht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/datenschutz/13489-rechtssichere-website-fuer-handwerker.html)

**"ZF Service Point" branding**
- ZF lists "ZF Service Point" as a partner category in its finder, and restructured its workshop concepts in February 2024 (Service Point, [pro]Tech CV variant and WABCO Service Partner merged into ZF [pro]Service for commercial vehicles). — [ZF Partner Finder](https://aftermarket.zf.com/partner-finder/); [ZF Press](https://press.zf.com/press/en/releases/release_65986.html)

### Inferences
- Safe pattern for titles/H1/ads: descriptive word use tied to the service — "DSG-Reparatur für VW, Audi, Seat, Skoda in Bochum", "Service für ZF-Automatikgetriebe (6HP/8HP)". Avoid: manufacturer logos, brand as first/dominant element or in the business/domain name, wording that suggests authorisation ("VW-Werkstatt", "Audi-Service" standing alone). Add "freie Kfz-Meisterwerkstatt" where a dealer relationship could be assumed. This follows from the BGH logo decision and § 23 commentary; the plain-word case was not found decided specifically for page titles or Google Ads.
- "ZF Service Point" and the ZF logo may be used only within the partner contract and ZF's brand guidelines; given the 2024 restructuring, get written confirmation of the current designation and logo files from ZF Aftermarket. Claiming a lapsed or mis-named partner status would be misleading advertising.
- Chiptuning pages, ads and videos should state for each offer whether a Teilegutachten/ABE exists, that an Änderungsabnahme and registration are required, the effect on manufacturer warranty and insurance, and should not advertise AGR/DPF/OPF/AdBlue deletes, pops-and-bangs or "TÜV-frei" at all. Performance figures should be supportable (dyno sheets).
- Reviews: never buy, write or reward-gate reviews; if testimonials or star averages are shown on the site, add a visible note on whether/how authenticity is checked (ProvenExpert/Google source naming alone may not suffice). Incentives for reviews (discount for 5 stars) were not found decided but sit close to Nr. 23c — avoid or have checked.
- Customers or creators who receive a discount, free service or payment for a video/post must label it as Werbung/Anzeige at the start; the workshop should require this in writing. Posts on the company's own clearly branded account need no extra label.
- Website hygiene (not sourced here, standard German practice): cookie consent under TDDDG before non-essential tags (Analytics, Ads, YouTube embeds), locally hosted fonts, privacy notice covering WhatsApp contact and contact forms, data-processing agreements with tool providers.

### Gaps
- Full reasoning of BGH I ZR 33/10 on plain word use (as opposed to the logo) not read; no decision found on brand names in SEO titles or Google Ads text/keywords for independent workshops (BGH/CJEU keyword-advertising case law not researched).
- OLG Stuttgart RaceChip case number/date need verification against the Wettbewerbszentrale PDF; no BGH ruling on chiptuning advertising found; no 2025–2026 decisions on emission-delete advertising found; § 27 EG-FGV / § 19 StVZO wording not checked at source.
- ZF logo/brand-use terms for partners not found.
- TDDDG cookie rules, Google Fonts and WhatsApp/DSGVO specifics for workshops not researched; whether the Meister title must be named in the Impressum should be checked with HWK Dortmund's guide.
- No case law found on review incentives (discount for review) or on customer-referral videos.
